How to play

Each player commands a small fleet on a 4 ft table. You score for ships still afloat, islands you hold and coins you keep.

Setup

Players take turns placing islands (12" from every edge, 6" apart), then rocks and reefs if you like. Each fleet lines up touching its own table edge, facing in. Player 1 goes first.

Your turn

First spend any coins you want. Then each of your ships takes its turn: one action, then it sails forward.

  • Set Heading. Turn up to 90° (Queen's Fleet: 180°).
  • Fire. Pick a cannon slot. The shot goes straight out from it, and a ship that fires can't turn this turn, so line up on an earlier turn. Tap when the ring is where you want the ball to land. The first ship it touches takes the hit.
  • Island. While touching an island: raise your flag (you must have been touching it at the end of your last turn, with no enemy ships touching it), collect a coin from an island you hold, or fire the island's gun in any direction.

Then click forward 1 to your Move Count (Queen's Fleet 3, Corsairs 4, and so on). You can't turn between clicks, and hitting a ship, island, rock or reef stops you there. An island action skips the click, so islands are the only place to hold still. A ship with no room at all at the table edge is scuttled. Ships you don't move sail on one click when you end the turn. Once every ship has gone, the turn ends by itself.

Cannons need a crew. Only a ship can fire, and a held island only fires when one of your ships is touching it and spends its action.

Damage

Each hit removes one fitting. Fittings always come off in this order: turret, then cargo, then masts (the Industry's smokestack counts as a mast). Repair puts them back the other way round, so a turret is the last thing back. With its turret gone, the Industry fires only its bow gun. A ship with no fittings is dead in the water: it can't move (it skips its click) but can still fire, and the next hit sinks it. You can scuttle your own dead ship so the enemy can't take it. No friendly fire.

Coins

You only get coins by collecting from islands you hold. Spend them at the start of your turn, before any ship acts. Coins you keep are worth a point each.

Winning

Last fleet afloat wins. Or, at the start of your turn, declare victory with 25 or more points and at least as many as everyone else. Points: 3 per ship, 2 per island, 1 per coin, and +2 for most ships, most islands and most coins (ties both get it). With the stalemate rule on, the game ends on points when every island is flagged and none has changed hands for two rounds.

Online

Up to seven crews on a round table, one turn each in seat order. The server checks every move and rolls every shot. If you drop out, reopen the link and you get your seat back.

Controls

Tap a ship, then an action. To move, drag the handle or tap the water to set the heading, then Sail. Pinch or use the wheel to zoom, drag empty water to pan. Arrow keys turn, Enter sails, Space fires, Esc cancels.
