# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: April 24, 2026_

## What we collect

When you subscribe to our mailing list, we collect the email address you
provide. That's it. We don't use analytics or advertising cookies on this
website.

## How we use it

Your email address is used solely to:

- Send you the printable rulebook booklet
- Notify you when new factions are released
- Share playtest notes and development updates

Our legal basis for processing your email address is your consent, given when
you subscribe.

## Third-party services

Email delivery is handled by [Kit](https://kit.com) (formerly ConvertKit),
acting as our data processor. When you subscribe, your email address is
stored on Kit's servers in the United States. Kit may use cookies or tracking
pixels to measure email opens and link clicks. Kit's privacy policy is
available at [kit.com/privacy](https://kit.com/privacy).

## Data retention

Your email address remains on our mailing list until you unsubscribe. We do
not sell, rent, or share your email address with anyone other than Kit as a
data processor.

## Your rights

Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link. You may unsubscribe at any
time, or email [josh@thederf.com](mailto:josh@thederf.com) to request
removal.

You also have the right to:

- Request a copy of the data we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data
- Receive your data in a portable format

EU and UK residents have the right to lodge a complaint with their local
data protection authority. California residents have equivalent rights under
the CCPA.

## Children's privacy

This mailing list is not intended for children under 16. If you believe we
have collected data from a child under 16, please email
[josh@thederf.com](mailto:josh@thederf.com) and we'll delete it.

## International users

Your data will be processed in the United States. This policy is governed by
the laws of the State of Georgia, United States.

## Changes to this policy

We'll update this policy if our practices change and update the "Last
updated" date above. Significant changes will be communicated via the
mailing list.

## Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email
[josh@thederf.com](mailto:josh@thederf.com).
