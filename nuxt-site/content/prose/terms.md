## Printing and playing safely

The pieces are small and the cannons fire small projectiles. The game is
meant for ages 10 and up, and younger kids should play with an adult
around. Don't aim at people's faces (or pets). You print the parts
yourself, so how well they turn out depends on your printer, material and
settings.
